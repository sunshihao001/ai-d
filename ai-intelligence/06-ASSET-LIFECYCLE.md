# 06｜Asset Lifecycle

A discovered item may remain temporary.

Possible states:

`DISCOVERED → CANDIDATE → RESEARCHING → EVIDENCE_CHECK → EXTRACTED → TESTING → ADOPTED`

Alternative outcomes:

`DEFERRED / UNKNOWN / REJECTED / DEPRECATED`

These are states, not mandatory stages.

## Asset types

- CONCEPT
- METHOD
- PATTERN
- SKILL_CANDIDATE
- WORKFLOW_CANDIDATE
- ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE
- INSIGHT
- EVIDENCE_CASE

## Important distinction

A Skill Candidate means:

> the AI has extracted a potentially reusable procedure.

It does **not** mean:

> the Skill is proven useful or production-ready.
