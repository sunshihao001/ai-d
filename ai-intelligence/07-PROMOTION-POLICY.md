# 07｜Promotion Policy

Nothing is promoted to long-term ai-d knowledge simply because it is interesting.

Before promotion, AI should establish as much as practical:

- problem relevance;
- source traceability;
- mechanism understanding;
- applicability;
- limitations;
- counter-evidence;
- actual validation when appropriate;
- expected reuse value.

## Promotion destinations

Possible destination:
- ai-d durable knowledge;
- a project-specific repository;
- a reusable Skill;
- a workflow;
- a research method;
- no promotion.

## Promotion principle

**Promotion is a decision, not a storage operation.**

If evidence is insufficient, keep the item as candidate/unknown/deferred.
