## Why these spaces exist

- sources: where something came from;
- discoveries: what was found before deeper work;
- research: active investigations;
- evidence: evidence bundles and counter-evidence;
- candidates: extracted reusable assets not yet adopted;
- experiments: real-world tests;
- decisions: adoption/rejection/defer decisions;
- archive: deprecated material kept only for traceability.

The folders are containers, not a mandatory research sequence.
