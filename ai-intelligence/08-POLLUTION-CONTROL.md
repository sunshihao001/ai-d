# 08｜Pollution Control

The main danger of this branch is turning large-scale discovery into large-scale contamination.

## Block immediate promotion of

- single-source claims;
- unverified social posts;
- marketing claims;
- README-only capability claims;
- theoretical analogies presented as engineering facts;
- old information with unknown current status;
- one successful experiment generalized into a permanent rule;
- duplicated summaries that add no new evidence or mechanism;
- AI self-assessment treated as independent validation.

## When a belief changes

Record:
1. previous judgment;
2. new evidence;
3. why the old judgment weakened;
4. new judgment;
5. affected assets;
6. whether downstream projects need rechecking.

## Deletion vs archive

Wrong or obsolete knowledge should normally be removed from active state, but its trace may be retained in archive when that helps prevent repeating the mistake.

The archive is not a second active knowledge base.
