# 05｜Evidence Policy

Every meaningful research conclusion should distinguish:

- **FACT** — directly supported by an inspectable source or runtime result.
- **OBSERVATION** — what was observed, without claiming generality.
- **INTERPRETATION** — an explanation of observed material.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — a proposed explanation or expectation not yet established.
- **INFERENCE** — a reasoned conclusion from multiple observations.
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence.
- **CONTRADICTED** — credible evidence conflicts with the claim.
- **VALIDATED** — tested sufficiently for the stated scope.

## Evidence strength is contextual

Do not create a universal numeric score before real research demonstrates that it is useful.

The first priority is transparent reasoning and traceability.
