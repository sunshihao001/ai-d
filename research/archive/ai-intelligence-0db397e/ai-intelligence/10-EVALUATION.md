# 10｜Evaluation

The branch should eventually be judged by whether it improves the user's actual AI use.

Primary measures:
- less repeated explanation;
- fewer repeated research tasks;
- better project decisions;
- better reusable Skills;
- lower unnecessary model cost;
- faster discovery of relevant mature solutions;
- fewer polluted/obsolete conclusions;
- better transfer from research to actual work.

Do not use:
- number of files;
- number of links;
- number of research rounds;
- amount of text;
- number of agents

as evidence that the system is better.

## First evaluation

Before large-scale ingestion, run a small controlled research experiment and compare:
- manual/ordinary research;
- the proposed AI-assisted process.

Only then decide which automation or scoring mechanisms deserve implementation.
