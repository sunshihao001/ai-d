# 09｜Automation Readiness

This branch must be ready for future automation, but automation should not be built before the information model is stable enough to test.

Future source inputs may include:
- X;
- GitHub;
- Reddit;
- web;
- official documentation;
- papers;
- books;
- other relevant communities.

Potential automation responsibilities:
- discovery;
- deduplication;
- clustering;
- source retrieval;
- evidence extraction;
- candidate ranking;
- research queueing;
- experiment scheduling;
- status updates;
- stale-item detection.

## Human control

Human intervention remains required for:
- changing long-term goals;
- major architecture changes;
- consequential external actions;
- irreversible operations;
- high-cost resource commitments.

Do not make human review a mandatory step for every low-risk discovery.
