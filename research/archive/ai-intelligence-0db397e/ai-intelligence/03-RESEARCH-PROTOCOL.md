# 03｜Research Protocol

Before external content is admitted, the AI should answer:

1. What problem or goal makes this worth looking at?
2. What exactly was discovered?
3. What source supports the discovery?
4. What is fact vs interpretation vs hypothesis?
5. Is there an implementation or independent evidence?
6. What alternatives or contradictory evidence exist?
7. What could be reused?
8. What remains unknown?
9. What would change the current conclusion?
10. Is further research worth the cost?

## Research behavior

Prefer:
- finished/open implementations;
- code, tests, issues, PRs and examples;
- multiple independent sources;
- actual user reports;
- primary documentation;
- runtime tests where feasible.

Avoid:
- article-summary chains;
- popularity as proof;
- README-only conclusions;
- model-generated claims without source support;
- endless research after the decision-relevant uncertainty is already resolved.

## Stopping rule

Research stops when:
- the decision is sufficiently supported;
- remaining uncertainty is explicitly recorded;
- further research is unlikely to change the decision;
- or the topic is intentionally deferred.
