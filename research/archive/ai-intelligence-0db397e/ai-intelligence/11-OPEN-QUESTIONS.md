# 11｜Open Questions

These are deliberately unresolved.

1. What is the cheapest useful division of labor between Muse, Hermes and stronger models?
2. Which source combinations produce the best signal for different AI topics?
3. How much autonomous filtering can be trusted before human correction becomes necessary?
4. What is the smallest useful evidence bundle?
5. When should a candidate become a Skill?
6. How should Skills be evaluated over time?
7. How should stale knowledge be detected?
8. How should AI learn from user corrections without turning every correction into a rigid global rule?
9. How should successful research feed back into future discovery?
10. Which parts should be automated first?

These questions should be answered by evidence and experiments, not by prematurely designing a large framework.
